1. Executive Summary

LaundTime is a full-stack, cloud-native urban laundry and garment logistics ecosystem designed to bridge consumers, local service partners/delivery riders, and platform administrators into one frictionless, real-time operating framework.

By uniting React, Vite, Node.js, Express, MongoDB Atlas, and unidirectional Server-Sent Events (SSE), LaundTime replaces traditional offline laundry bottlenecks with transparent per-piece & weight pricing, live doorstep tracking, digital wallet checkouts, and a persistent AI-powered Fabric Care Assistant.

4. Movable & Circular AI Garment Care Assistant

LaundTime includes a built-in interactive AI assistant engineered specifically for fabric recommendations and voice-assisted customer service:

  • 62px Circular Bubble: Styled with modern gradient and animated live green pulse indicator.
  • Free Drag-and-Drop Mobility: Draggable anywhere across the viewport using unified Pointer Events (desktop mouse and mobile touch).
  • Smart Dynamic Viewport Anchoring: Automatically positions the chat popover cleanly above or below the bubble depending on screen coordinates so it never overflows off-screen.
  • Position Memory: Persists user placement in localStorage across refreshes.
  • Voice Speech Recognition: Built-in Web Speech API enables hands-free voice inquiries in English and Indian accents.

5. Business Model & Monetization Strategy

The platform operates on a robust, multi-stream urban commerce monetization model:

  • Per-Order Service Commission (15%–20%): Captured on every completed transaction across partner washing facilities and delivery fleets.
  • Recurring KG & VIP Subscriptions: Pre-paid monthly laundry plans offering predictable recurring revenue and customer retention.
  • Express Surcharge: Premium markup for guaranteed 6-hour emergency turnaround wash & dry.
  • B2B Commercial Accounts: Institutional contracts for boutique hotels, fitness clubs, and serviced apartments.
Technical Highlight: Built with zero hardcoded local dependencies, resilient DNS SRV resolvers, and automatic guest-order customer auto-enrollment to guarantee 100% order capture into cloud MongoDB Atlas.
